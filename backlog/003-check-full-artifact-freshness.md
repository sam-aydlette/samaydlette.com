Diagnosed 2026-08-24 during Session 2's verification-interface work and recorded as an open gap in `~/.claude/setup/SETUP-LOG.md` ("Stale-artifact freshness gap ... Real gap, not closed").

The problem in one line: `make check-full` guards artifact **presence** and calls that a gate, while the invariant that would guard artifact **coherence** turns itself off in exactly the configuration `check-full` uses.

Why it matters now rather than later: Sessions 4–6 make agent work land on the strength of a green gate, unattended, overnight. A gate that reports green while three of its ten invariants are inert is the kind of thing that is fine until the night it is not.

Two levels of fix. Do the first; the second is a judgment call to make in the PR:

1. **Honesty (do this).** Have `check-full` print exactly which invariants this invocation does *not* exercise, and why, before it runs. Cheap, no behaviour change, and it removes the false equivalence between local green and CI green. Pair it with the fixture test in ACCEPTANCE so the coherence check is proven to work at all.

2. **Enforcement (decide in the PR).** Derive the expected commit locally — the artifacts carry `provenance.source.commit`, so `check-full` can pass `--expect-commit $(git rev-parse HEAD)` when the tree is clean — and let (f) actually run. The catch, and the reason this is not obviously right: a developer with a dirty tree, or one who built artifacts before their last commit, would then get a red `check-full` for a reason that has nothing to do with their change. That may be correct (the artifacts really are stale) or it may be noise. If it turns out to be noise, an alternative is to require only that the four artifacts agree with *each other* on `signal_id` / commit, without pinning them to `HEAD` — that catches the mixed-vintage set without punishing an uncommitted edit.

Whichever route: state the reasoning in the PR body, and do not reach for the option that makes the check quieter.
