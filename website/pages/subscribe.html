What is RSS?

RSS is a simple, decades-old way for a website to announce new posts. A free app called a feed reader checks the sites you follow and shows you anything new in one place. Nothing is tracked, and you can unsubscribe at any time by removing the site from your reader.

How to subscribe

  1. Open a feed reader. Many are free, on the web, on your phone, or built into your email app or browser.
  2. Choose the option to add a feed or subscription.
  3. Paste this address:

https://samaydlette.com/feed.xml

Prefer to just browse? Every article is listed on the Articles page.
